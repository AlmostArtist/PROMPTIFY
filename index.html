What PROMPTIFY does
with your data.

01 / DATA

Information we collect

PROMPTIFY is built to need as little of your data as possible. Here's exactly what it touches.

Content you choose to process

When you run a tool — Enhance, Summarize, Translate, Brainstorm, Code Review, Image Prompt, and so on — the extension reads the relevant text. That's whatever you type into the side panel, content from the tab you've focused, or text pulled from a PDF or DOCX file you open in it, so it can be sent for AI processing.

Your OpenRouter API key

PROMPTIFY is "bring your own key." The OpenRouter API key you enter in Settings is stored locally in your browser and used only to authenticate your own requests to OpenRouter. We never receive or see this key.

Optional CLI account details

If you enable the native companion, the official Codex or Claude CLI may report limited display details such as email, plan, login method, organization, CLI version, model availability, and quota windows. PROMPTIFY uses these details only in the local Connections screen. Passwords, session tokens, and raw authentication output never cross the native bridge.

Saved prompts and history

Prompts you save, and your enhancement history, are stored locally in your browser. This data stays on your device — we don't receive a copy.

What we do not collect

  • Account registration details, names, or email addresses
  • Payment or financial information
  • Health information
  • Precise location data
  • Analytics or browsing-tracking data

02 / USAGE

How information is used

  • Text you submit is sent to the provider you select: OpenRouter through its API, or the official Codex/Claude CLI through the optional local native companion. We do not run a PROMPTIFY server that receives, logs, or stores this content.
  • Saved prompts, history, and your API key remain in local browser storage so the extension works across sessions on your device.
  • File text (PDF/DOCX) is parsed locally in your browser to extract text before it's used in a tool you choose to run.

03 / THIRD PARTIES

Third-party services

PROMPTIFY sends content only when you actively run an AI tool. Depending on your selected connection, the recipient is OpenRouter, OpenAI through Codex CLI, or Anthropic through Claude Code. Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Review your selected provider's privacy policy before sending sensitive content. PROMPTIFY does not control how OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, or routed model providers handle data.

04 / SHARING

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising, and we do not use it for credit, lending, or insurance decisions. Period.

05 / STORAGE

Storage and security

Everything PROMPTIFY remembers — your API key, saved prompts, history, and settings — is stored locally on your device via Chrome's storage API. Uninstalling the extension removes this data. We don't maintain a backend database of your content.

06 / CONTROL

Your choices and controls

  • Clear your enhancement history or delete saved prompts any time, from inside the extension.
  • Remove your OpenRouter API key any time — this disables AI-powered features.
  • Uninstall the extension any time to remove all locally stored data.

07 / PERMISSIONS

Permissions explained

Each permission PROMPTIFY requests maps to one specific feature — nothing is requested "just in case."

Permission Why we need it
storage Save your prompts, history, and settings locally on your device.
sidePanel Display the PROMPTIFY interface in Chrome's side panel.
activeTab / scripting Read text from, and insert generated text into, the AI chat tab you're actively using.
downloads Let you export results as a .md file.
contextMenusOffer explicitly selected AI actions from Chrome's context menu.
nativeMessagingTalk to the optional, locally installed CLI companion.
optional: history / tabsEnable browser recall and tab actions only after you approve access.
host: openrouter.aiSend requests to OpenRouter using your own API key.

08 / AGE

Children's privacy

PROMPTIFY is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
